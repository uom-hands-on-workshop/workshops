## Activity 1 

### Open Challenge

#### Step 1: Discuss with your partner (5 mins)

**Objective**: Identify perceived limitations of generative AI technologies.

**Instructions**:

- Pair up with someone near you.
- Discuss the following:
  - What are some of the **current limitations** of generative AI?
  - Choose **2–3 limitations** that you and your partner find most significant.
- Don’t worry about writing full answers – this step is just to **think critically** about the tech and exchange ideas.

#### Step 2: Test the boundaries (5 mins)

**Objective**: Investigate whether the limitations identified are still present in other genAI tools.

**Instructions**:

- Explore how genAI tools perform against the limitations you identified
    - Are the limitations still valid?
    - Can the tools **overcome** the limitations you identified?
    - Do some tools perform better in specific areas?
    - Are there **new limitations** that emerged as you experimented?

**Tips**:

- Try giving similar prompts across tools to compare responses.
- Share any surprises or unexpected outcomes during the group reflection.

_If you finish early or are unable to identify a current limitation, [you can explore several examples here](activities4_1)_
